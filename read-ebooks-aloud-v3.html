Questions people actually ask

Can PhonoLeaf read any ebook?

PhonoLeaf reads DRM-free EPUB files. Connect the Google Drive folder they are in and they appear in your library automatically, including ones you add later.

Does the voice sound robotic?

No. PhonoLeaf uses a natural neural voice generated on your device, rather than the flat system voice most reading apps fall back on.

Can I change the reading speed?

Yes, anywhere from 0.5× to 2×, adjustable at any time without losing your place.
