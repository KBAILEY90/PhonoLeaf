Questions people actually ask

Does Google Play Books' read aloud work on every book?

Not always. Publishers choose whether Read Aloud is enabled for books bought in the store, though it also works on your own uploaded DRM-free EPUB or PDF files. Its natural voice needs an internet connection, and you can switch to the basic on-device voice for offline use. PhonoLeaf reads any DRM-free EPUB in your Google Drive, offline, in one natural voice throughout.

Can PhonoLeaf read books I did not buy from Google or Apple?

Yes. PhonoLeaf reads any DRM-free EPUB file, wherever it came from: public domain titles, DRM-free purchases, or your own files, as long as it is in the folder you connect.

Does PhonoLeaf work offline?

Yes. You can save a book for offline listening, and the on-device natural voice keeps working with no network connection at all.
