Frequently asked questions

Can PhonoLeaf read any ebook?

PhonoLeaf reads DRM-free EPUB files. Connect the Google Drive folder they're in and they appear in your library automatically.

Does the voice sound robotic?

No. PhonoLeaf uses a natural neural voice generated on your device, not the flat system voice most reading apps rely on.

Can I change the reading speed?

Yes, from 0.5x to 2x, adjustable any time.

Is PhonoLeaf free to try?

Yes, a free 7-day trial with full access, then $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year.
