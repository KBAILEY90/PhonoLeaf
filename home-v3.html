How PhonoLeaf uses your Google Drive data

PhonoLeaf asks for read-only access to Google Drive (the drive.readonly scope). It uses that access for exactly one purpose: to list the ebook files (EPUB) in the folder you choose and download them so they can be rendered and read aloud.

What PhonoLeaf never does
  • Never creates, edits, moves or deletes anything in your Drive: the access it holds is read-only.
  • Never sends your files or your Drive data to any server of ours. Your books travel from Google straight to your device.
  • Never uses your data for advertising, and never sells or transfers it.
  • Never opens anything outside the folder you pick. The permission Google grants is broader than that, because it is the only one that lets new books appear without you re-picking them each time, but PhonoLeaf only ever lists and downloads the ebooks in the one folder you connect.

Your reading position, listening stats and settings are stored on your own device, not in an account. Signing out revokes PhonoLeaf's access to your Google account. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
