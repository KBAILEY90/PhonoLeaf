Frequently asked questions

Does PhonoLeaf sound more natural than @Voice?

PhonoLeaf uses a natural neural voice generated on your device, rather than your phone's built-in system text-to-speech voice, which is what apps like @Voice rely on.

Is PhonoLeaf private?

Yes. Your ebooks are read aloud entirely on your device. PhonoLeaf has no backend that receives or stores your book content, and your files travel straight from your Google Drive to your device.

What platforms does PhonoLeaf run on?

Any modern browser, on phone, tablet or desktop, plus a dedicated Android app. It can be installed to your home screen like an app.

How much does it cost?

$5.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a free 7-day trial.
