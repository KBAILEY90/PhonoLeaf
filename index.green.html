PhonoLeaf

A voice for every page

Turn the ebooks already sitting in your Google Drive into an audiobook library.

1
Connect a Drive folder
Pick the folder your ebooks are in. That's the only one PhonoLeaf reads.
2
Your library appears
Every ebook in that folder, with its real cover art. Add more later and they show up on their own.
3
Press play
A natural voice reads aloud, even with the screen off, and remembers where you stopped.

Read-only access. PhonoLeaf never changes, uploads or deletes anything in your Drive. Voices are generated on your phone, and there's no server. Your books go from Google straight to your device.

About the natural voice

    

      PhonoLeaf reads using one of its own natural-sounding voice models,
      generated entirely on your device — nothing you read is ever sent
      to a server to be turned into speech.
    

    

      Capable devices get the Upgraded voice; other devices automatically
      get the Standard voice instead — PhonoLeaf picks based on how
      fast your device can generate speech. Only if neither can keep up,
      which is rare, does it briefly fall back to your phone’s
      Built-in voice, just so reading never stops.
    

    

      Retry re-tests this device, which is worth doing if it happened to
      be busy the first time.
