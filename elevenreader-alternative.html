Frequently asked questions

How much does PhonoLeaf cost compared to ElevenReader?

PhonoLeaf is $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a free 7-day trial. ElevenReader's Ultra plan is $11 a month or $99 a year.

Is PhonoLeaf really private?

Yes. Your ebooks are read aloud entirely on your device. PhonoLeaf has no backend that receives or stores your book content, and your files travel straight from your Google Drive to your device.

Does PhonoLeaf work offline?

Yes, fully. You can save a book for offline listening, and the voice itself is generated on your device, so it keeps working without a network connection at all.

Does PhonoLeaf have a free plan?

PhonoLeaf offers a free 7-day trial with full access, then continues as a paid subscription.
