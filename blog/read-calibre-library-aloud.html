How to read your Calibre library aloud

No plugin required, just a folder
Published August 19, 2026

Calibre is where a lot of careful readers keep their library organized, tagged, and converted into whatever format they need. What it isn't built to do is read any of it back to you in a voice worth listening to.

Calibre organizes, it doesn't narrate

Calibre's own viewer does have a basic read-aloud option, but it relies on whatever text-to-speech voice is already installed on your computer, the same flat, mechanical voice built into your operating system. It's functional, not something you'd choose to listen to for a whole novel.

Getting your library into PhonoLeaf

PhonoLeaf doesn't plug directly into Calibre. It connects to a Google Drive folder, so bridging the two takes one extra step, not an ongoing one.

  1. Find your Calibre library folder It's a plain folder of EPUB files on your computer, whatever you pointed Calibre at when you set it up.
  2. Copy or sync it to Google Drive Drag the folder into Drive, or point Drive's desktop sync app at it. Either way, this is a one-time setup, not something you repeat per book.
  3. Connect that folder in PhonoLeaf Sign in with Google and choose the folder. Every DRM-free EPUB inside becomes something you can press play on.
A note on DRM Calibre libraries can hold books picked up from all kinds of sources, and some of those may still be DRM-protected. PhonoLeaf only opens DRM-free EPUBs, same as any standard EPUB reader would.

Frequently asked questions

Does PhonoLeaf integrate directly with Calibre?

Not directly. PhonoLeaf connects to a Google Drive folder, so getting a Calibre library into it takes one bridge step: copying or syncing your Calibre folder to Drive.

Will my Calibre metadata, like covers, carry over?

PhonoLeaf reads a book's own embedded EPUB metadata and cover directly from the file, so covers and titles generally show up correctly without any extra step.

What about DRM-protected books in my Calibre library?

PhonoLeaf only opens DRM-free EPUBs. Books in your Calibre library that are still protected won't work, the same as they wouldn't in any other EPUB reader without the protection removed.

Do I need to keep re-copying files every time?

No. Once the folder is connected, anything you add to it later appears in PhonoLeaf automatically, so it's really a one-time setup rather than an ongoing chore.
