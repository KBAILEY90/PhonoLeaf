Questions people actually ask

Is PhonoLeaf really private?

Yes. Your ebooks are read aloud entirely on your device. PhonoLeaf has no backend that receives or stores your book content, and your files travel straight from your Google Drive to your device.

Does PhonoLeaf work offline?

Yes. You can save a book for offline listening, and the on-device voice keeps working with no network connection at all.

Can PhonoLeaf read my own EPUB files?

Yes, for DRM-free EPUB files. Connect the Google Drive folder they are in and they appear in your library automatically, including new ones you add later.

How much does PhonoLeaf cost?

$5.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a free 7-day trial. There is also a one-time Founding Member option at $129, limited to the first 500 buyers. See the Terms of Service for full billing details.
