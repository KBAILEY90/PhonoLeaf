A voice for every page

PhonoLeaf uses the ebooks already sitting in your Google Drive to build an audiobook library. Connect a folder once, and every ebook in it becomes something you can listen to, on a walk, doing the dishes, or on the commute.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google Used only to reach the ebook files in your own Drive. PhonoLeaf has no separate account to create.
  2. Choose the Drive folder your ebooks live in Browse your Drive inside the app and pick a folder. Add more ebooks to it later and they simply appear — no re-picking.
  3. Press play Pick a book and listen. PhonoLeaf remembers exactly where you stopped, in every book.

What it does

Natural speech A neural voice that runs entirely on your device, with a choice of accents and voices. Falls back to your system voice where that isn't available.
Keeps reading with the screen off Playback continues when your phone is locked, with play/pause, page and chapter controls on the lock screen.
Picks up where you left off Your position is saved per book, down to the paragraph — across pauses, chapters and restarts.
Your library, as a library Real cover art pulled from each ebook, search, chapter jumping and adjustable reading speed from 0.5× to 2×.
Listening stats Hours listened, a day streak, and a breakdown by author, book, genre or book length.
Light and dark A warm paper theme by day, a dim forest one by night, or follow your system setting.

How PhonoLeaf uses your Google Drive data

PhonoLeaf asks for read-only access to Google Drive (the drive.readonly scope). It uses that access for exactly one purpose: to list the ebook files (EPUB) in the folder you choose and download them so they can be rendered and read aloud.

What PhonoLeaf never does
  • Never creates, edits, moves or deletes anything in your Drive — the access it holds is read-only.
  • Never sends your files or your Drive data to any server of ours. PhonoLeaf has no backend: your books travel from Google straight to your device.
  • Never uses your data for advertising, and never sells or transfers it.
  • Never reads anything outside the folder you connect, in normal use.

Your reading position, listening stats and settings are stored locally on your own device, not in an account. Signing out revokes PhonoLeaf's access to your Google account. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Where you can use it

PhonoLeaf runs in the browser on phones, tablets and desktops, and can be installed to your home screen as an app. An Android build is currently in private testing.
