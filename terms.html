What the app does

PhonoLeaf connects to a folder in your own Google Drive, reads the epub files it finds there, and reads them aloud using on-device or your device's built-in text-to-speech. The app has no server or backend — it runs entirely on your device and talks directly to Google's own services.

  • The app only ever reads the folder you explicitly connect — it does not access any other part of your Drive.
