About PhonoLeaf

Any book, anywhere, any time.

That is what PhonoLeaf is for.

Why it exists

Reading is a good thing that asks an awkward favour. You have to sit down, hold the book, and find the time. Most days that time never quite arrives, and the book stays where you left it.

PhonoLeaf removes that condition. The books you already own become something you can read anywhere you happen to be: on the bus, in the car, on the train, at home, out in the woods, on a paddle board, or drifting somewhere off the coast on whatever happens to float. If you can listen, you can read.

Where it is going

PhonoLeaf should be the leader in the audiobook market, by making any book you own reachable anywhere, at any time. Not a catalogue you rent from, and not a subscription to somebody else's library. Your own books, read to you, wherever you are.

Who builds it

PhonoLeaf is built independently, by one person, in Québec. There is no company behind it, no investors, and no growth team. That is also why it has no server: the app runs on your device because that is the honest way to build it, not because it tested well.
