What connecting a folder actually does

Read-only, always PhonoLeaf only ever reads the folder you choose — it can't create, edit, move, or delete anything in your Drive.
New books appear automatically Add another EPUB to the folder later and it shows up in your library on its own — no re-picking, no re-importing.
Nothing sent to a server PhonoLeaf has no backend for your books — files go straight from Google Drive to your device, and the voice is generated locally.
Real covers, not placeholders Each book's actual cover art is pulled from the file itself and cached for fast browsing.

Frequently asked questions

Does PhonoLeaf need full access to my Google Drive?

No — it asks for read-only access (the drive.readonly scope), and only ever reads the one folder you connect.

What happens if I add a new book to the folder?

It appears in your PhonoLeaf library automatically, the next time you open the app — no re-picking or re-importing.

Is my Google account data safe?

Yes — sign-in is used only to reach the ebook files in your own Drive. PhonoLeaf has no separate account to create, and signing out revokes its access to your Google account.

How much does PhonoLeaf cost?

$5.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a free 7-day trial.
