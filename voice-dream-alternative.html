Frequently asked questions

How much does PhonoLeaf cost compared to Voice Dream Reader?

PhonoLeaf is $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a free 7-day trial. Voice Dream Reader's subscription is $79.99 a year.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can save a book for offline listening, and the on-device voice keeps working without a network connection.

Is PhonoLeaf good for dyslexia or ADHD?

Many readers use PhonoLeaf for exactly that: a calm, natural voice, adjustable reading speed from 0.5x to 2x, and picking up exactly where you left off, in every book.

What platforms does PhonoLeaf run on?

Any modern browser — phone, tablet or desktop — and a dedicated Android app. It can be installed to your home screen like an app.
